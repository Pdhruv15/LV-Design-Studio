## Studies

| # | Module | What it does | Main files |
|---|---|---|---|
| 08 | [Voltage drop](08-voltage-drop.md) | Works out the source-to-load voltage drop of every panel-to-panel and equipment cable (optionally each DB's worst final circuit) by the R + X method: k x I x L x (R cos phi + X sin phi) / runs, with k = sqrt3 (3-phase) or 2 (1-phase), R corrected for conductor temperature, incomer PF taken from downstream P and Q per phase, and the upstream drops added. Totals are checked against one project limit, and motors also against a starting-drop limit. Results go to the page, CSV and a black-and-white PDF. The core copy in evaluateFeeder feeds the cable schedule, the calc report and the SLD. | `src/calc/voltageDrop.ts`, `src/calc/electrical.ts`, `src/calc/cableTable.ts`, `src/calc/motor.ts` +8 more |
| 09 | [Earthing & earth fault loop](09-earthing.md) | Earth fault loop study (TN-S only): Zs = Ze + (R1 + R2), minimum If = 0.95 U0 / Zs against the instantaneous trip or 5 x IΔn, 0.4 s / 5 s per IEC 60364-4-41, and an adiabatic CPC check with k = 143. A separate earthing schematic lays out RMU, transformer, LV and generator earth pits with site tests against DEWA limits, plus earth riser and floor earthing sheets. The two parts share only cpcOf and the board-sheet line (boardEarth); | `src/calc/earthing.ts`, `src/calc/cableTable.ts`, `src/calc/electrical.ts`, `src/calc/runs.ts` +7 more |
| 10 | [Protection coordination](10-protection-coordination.md) | Draws generic (non-manufacturer) log-log time-current curves. | 11 | [Breaker & cable selection](11-breaker-cable-selection.md) | Checks and recommends each circuit's breaker (In, type, Icu) and cable (size, parallel runs) using Ib <= In <= Iz, an 85 % loading margin, a voltage-drop budget and Icu >= busbar fault. Iz comes from the cable data (built-in reference or Cables.xlsx) for the IEC 60364-5-52 installation method with ambient / ground, soil and grouping factors. The same recommend() is reused by every auto-sizing path in the app. | `src/components/studies/SelectionStudy.tsx`, `src/calc/sizing.ts`, `src/calc/electrical.ts`, `src/calc/installation.ts` +7 more |
| 12 | [Transformer & generator](12-transformer-generator.md) | Sizes one transformer per main board from the network demand (boardTotals, after optional planned PFC, growth and loading limit) with FLC, ACB, LV fault, regulation and bus-coupler outage checks, and sizes the standby generator from whole boards with a largest-motor-start check (X'd 25 %, dip 15 %). It also checks parallel transformers (load share by kVA/uk, coupler current), describes ATS / interlocked changeovers for the SLD and BOQ, and builds the DEWA TCL summary at transformer level. Chosen sizes can be written back to the main board (kVA, Z %, busbar) and the standby board (kVA). | `src/calc/txGen.ts`, `src/calc/sizing.ts`, `src/calc/parallelTx.ts`, `src/calc/motor.ts` +9 more |
| 13 | [Power factor correction](13-power-factor-correction.md) | Sizes capacitor banks to the project PF target with Qc = P(tan φ1 − tan φ2): a design study on the network (central, group or individual, bottom-up, with steps, detuning, breaker ≥ 1.43 × In and cable) and a standalone calculator for existing installations with its own PDF report. Banks can be written back to the SLD, where an APFC switching rule counts only the switched kvar in all built-in totals. | `src/calc/pfc.ts`, `src/calc/pfcCalc.ts`, `src/calc/capSwitching.ts`, `src/components/studies/PfcStudy.tsx` +6 more |
| 14 | [Busbar & busway](14-busbar.md) | Sizes busbar trunking risers for high-rise buildings (demand with diversity, In, ambient derating, Cu/Al selection from a data table, floor-by-floor voltage drop, Icw vs source fault level, size, weight, BOQ parts) and checks a board's typed busbar Icw against its fault level. | `src/calc/busbar.ts`, `src/calc/busbarIcw.ts`, `src/components/studies/BusbarStudy.tsx`, `src/components/BoardFields.tsx` +9 more |
| 15 | [UPS & solar PV](15-ups-solar.md) | Sizes each UPS from a UPS board's demand or a typed load list. The UPS must cover both kVA and kW (output PF).
