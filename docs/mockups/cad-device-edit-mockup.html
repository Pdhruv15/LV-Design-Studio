Design CAD → Plan → Devices → Edit — mockup (2026-11-06)

Click a device (or drag a window round several): a small toolbar opens beside it with the edits that suit what is picked — a light, a socket, a smoke detector, the DB, or several at once. Right-click gives the same list as a menu at the cursor. The side panel keeps the details and warnings.

Tools to change many devices at once instead of one by one. Select several devices with a window, then align them, space them equally, replace them with another device, move them to another circuit or match another device. Array on line places N devices evenly along a line you draw (a corridor of lights). Every change updates the point counts on the circuits and the load schedule, as Add device does today, and is one undo step.

    Notes for the decision
    • Toolbar at the cursor: opens just above what is picked (below it near the top edge), hides while a window is dragged, and closes with Esc or a click elsewhere. What it offers: Light — Array on line, Replace, Circuit, Match, Move, Copy, Delete · socket / other LV point — Replace, Circuit, Match, Move, Copy, Delete · ELV device — Replace (same system), Coverage, Match, Move, Copy, Delete · Board — Rename, Circuits…, Move, Delete · several — Align row, Align column, Space equally, then what all of them have in common; a mixed set adds Filter (Lights only…). Replace and Circuit open a short list right there, Circuit with the point count after the change.
    • Right-click a device: the same options as a menu at the cursor.
    • Selecting: drag left to right selects what is fully inside; right to left also what the window crosses (as in AutoCAD). Shift-click adds or removes one device. Esc clears. A filter in the panel (Lights only, Sockets only…) narrows a mixed selection.
    • Align in a row / column: the devices take the average Y (row) or X (column). Space equally keeps the two outer devices and spreads the rest evenly between them.
    • Replace: LV points can become another LV point type, ELV devices another device of the same system. A type that does not suit the circuit (a socket on a lighting circuit) is warned, not blocked.
    • Circuit: the points move to the other circuit; the panel shows both circuits' point counts after the change, and warns if the new circuit goes over its point limit.
    • Move, Copy, Delete work on the whole selection (today one device at a time). Also Rotate for device symbols? Symbols are drawn upright today — not in this first version.
    • Selecting drawn lines, trays and routes with the same window comes with the core edit tools (Trim, Extend, Offset…) proposed next.
