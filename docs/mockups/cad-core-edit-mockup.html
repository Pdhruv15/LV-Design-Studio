Design CAD → Draw → Modify / Edit — core edit tools mockup (2026-11-06)

The CAD edit tools engineers expect, for what is drawn in the app: lines, polylines, cable trays and cable routes. The architect's drawing is never changed, but its walls can be the cutting edge for Trim and the boundary for Extend. Pick a drawn object (click, or a window as for devices) and a toolbar beside it offers the edits that suit it; the same tools are on the ribbon and the command line with their usual AutoCAD names.

Try it: Trim — click the part of the arrowed line that sticks through a wall · Extend — click near the short line's end · Offset — type 0.3, click the tray, then the side · Fillet — click the two cable lines · or click any drawn line for its toolbar and drag its square grips.

Notes for the decision
ToolCommandWhat it does (on what is drawn in the app)
TrimTRClick the part to cut away; it is cut at the nearest crossing lines — drawn lines or the architect's walls (no edges to pick first, as AutoCAD's quick mode). Shift-click extends instead.
ExtendEXClick near an end; it runs on to the next line in its direction, drawn or a wall. Shift-click trims instead.
OffsetOType the distance (or T = through a point), click the object, click the side. A tray offsets as a tray of the same width and service; a cable route as a line.
Fillet / ChamferF / CHATwo lines: the corner is joined with a radius (0 = sharp corner) or cut at a distance. On a tray run the radius is the bend's.
Break / Join / ExplodeBR / J / XBreak a line or route at a point (or between two); join lines and polylines that meet end to end into one; explode a polyline or rectangle into lines.
Mirror / Scale / StretchMI / SC / SMirror about a line (keep the original: yes / no); scale about a base point by a factor; stretch moves the points inside a crossing window, so a tray run gets longer while its far end stays put.
ArrayARRectangular (rows × columns, spacing) or polar (number, angle) copies of drawn objects. Devices use Array on line (ARL, PR 424).
Grips—A drawn object picked shows square grips at its ends and corners and a grip at each mid-point: drag an end to move it, a mid-point to move that side.
  • Toolbar beside what is picked, as for devices: a line or route — Trim, Extend, Offset, Break, Fillet, Mirror, Move, Copy, Rotate, Erase; a tray — Offset, Break, Fillet (bend), Width ▾, Move, Copy, Erase; several — Join, Mirror, Scale, Array, Move, Copy, Erase. Right-click gives the same as a menu.
  • Window selection picks drawn objects and devices together; a filter in the toolbar keeps only one kind.
  • Trays and cable routes keep their tray route, cable and width through every edit, so Update schedules gives the new lengths and fittings (a fillet becomes a bend).
  • Ribbon: Mirror, Scale and Stretch join Modify; Trim … Array are a new Edit group on the Draw sub-tab. Each is one undo step.
