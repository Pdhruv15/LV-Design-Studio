Mockup: dashed frame around the main board, earth pits outside, vertical MET (SLD, DEWA style off)

When the DEWA panel frame is off, one dashed frame encloses the board at the top of the SLD, as the DEWA frame does. That is the MDB, or the DB or SMDB when it is the SLD's only board. Inside are the vertical MET, incomer breaker, board name, busbar and outgoing breakers. The LV earth pits and their test links (one per pit) stand outside the frame, on the left. The rounded padded MDB box is removed, and the name and results are plain text. The transformer and its N/body earths stay outside, above the frame. It replaces the pit-only frame from PR 509. The yellow callouts are for this mockup only and are not drawn on the SLD.

Details:
• Same dash as the transformer body box (4 / 3). Black-and-white in PDF and DXF exports, like every other line.
• The incomer crosses the frame top and the outgoing cables cross the bottom, each with a gland mark, as in the DEWA frame.
• Boards fed from another board (sub-DBs under an MDB) keep their current look.
• Only when the DEWA panel frame is off (standard style, or a DEWA main board with no outgoing panels). The DEWA framed panel is unchanged.
• One frame per main board. With two transformer-fed boards side by side, each gets its own frame and the frames never touch.
• Each pit is tested on its own at its test link; losing one conductor leaves the other pit connected (as the DEWA style already draws).
• Outside the frame the pits get more room: 6.0 m (MIN) reads below its dimension line, clear of the earth symbols.
No title on the frame: the board name is already inside it.
