The route from the selected board or load back to its transformer is drawn over the SLD in the accent colour, with the running voltage drop at each board. The steps are listed beside it. Only results the app already calculates are shown.

Run and checkRun shows the number of circuits out of date under its name; today that is only in the tooltip. Check is the fail and to-check count of the results drawer: one press goes to the next problem circuit. Trace is new and shows why it fails (4).
EquipmentThe Supply and Devices menus (ACB, MCCB, MCB, isolator, kWh meter, CT metering, RCD, SPD, capacitor bank) pick the same items as the equipment library: choose one, then click the board or the empty canvas. Add load is unchanged.
ResultsThe three lists of the View menu above the canvas, each with its current setting beside it, so what the drawing is showing can be read without opening a menu. Phase balance and Discrimination would be two new Colour by choices.
Copy and undo · ViewCopy, Paste, Undo and Redo work today from the keyboard only. Fit is on the canvas toolbar. Duplicate and Find do not exist yet and appear only when built. Find zooms to the board, feeder or load it finds.
Output · MoreExport gathers the drawing export from the bar above the canvas and the OpenDSS export from More. More keeps Load flow, Quick calcs, Calculation report and Project settings.
Narrow windowsToday the row scrolls sideways. Proposed: groups that do not fit move into More under their own headings. Every button of today's ribbon stays on the row at 1366 px.
