Whole-border template, logo area and the legend table in Design CAD

Mockup of 2026-11-10 for the three pieces of the 2026-11-09 proposal still open after v2.067: the whole-border mode (D4 of the title block note), a logo area in a drawn block, and step 5, the legend as a table object placed by hand on a layout. Purple dashes mark what is new. Nothing in it is built; each section ends with the decisions it needs.

1Whole-border template: the company's frame replaces the app's approval

A template whose outline is a whole paper (A3 420 × 297, A1 841 × 594 …) is a border. On a sheet of that paper the app draws no frame, no zone strip and no title band of its own: the border block sits at the paper's origin, its own grid references and title strip print, and the app's fields fill its attributes as they do in a block today. Everything the app adds (drawing, legend column, notes, revision rows) stays inside the border's drawing area.

How it fits what exists

  • Import. A DWG whose block (or whose whole model space, when there is no block) measures a paper size within 2 mm is offered as "Whole border for A3"; the user can untick it and keep it as a block in the corner, as today.
  • Per paper, not per sheet kind. The border is chosen by paper size (A3, A2, A1, A0, A4). A sheet whose paper has no border prints with the app's frame and the block chosen for its kind. The border is never scaled.
  • Drawing area. Detected once at import (the largest empty rectangle inside the outline that touches no text), kept with the template, and movable in the designer. Fit, autoSize, viewports on layouts and the legend column take it as the frame's inside.
  • Revision rows. New tags {Revn} {RevDaten} {RevTextn} {RevByn}; rows past the issues stay blank. A border without them keeps the app's revision table in the column (set's "Revision rows" setting).
  • Edit in CAD. A border opens at 1 : 1 with no reference A3 frame (it is its own); TB-REF shows the drawing area as a dashed rectangle the user can move or resize; TBSAVE keeps the paper and the area.
  • DWG / DXF. One INSERT at 0,0 with the attribute values; E-FRAME carries only the paper outline. PDF unchanged in colour (black and white).

Decisions

  • B1 One border per paper size, other papers fall back to the app frame (recommended), or scale the nearest border?
  • B2 Drawing area detected with a manual override (recommended), or always set by hand?
  • B3 Revision rows through tags, app table otherwise (recommended), or always the app's table in the column?

2Logo area in a drawn block approval

a · Place it in Design CAD

The Properties palette's field chips gain Logo area: two clicks draw a rectangle on TB-FIELD labelled {Logo}. One per block; grips resize it. An imported DWG offers one where it finds an IMAGE entity or a rectangle on a layer named LOGO.

b · On the printed sheet

The project's logo (Project → Drawing, a raster image as today) prints inside the rectangle, centred and in proportion; no rectangle is drawn around it unless the block has one. No logo set: the area stays empty.

c · In the DWG / DXF

The engine writes no raster images yet. Recommended: the DWG's block carries a LOGO attribute showing the company name at the area, so the CAD team can drop their own logo there. The alternative, a vector logo, works today: draw it with lines in the block.

  • L1 In DWG, the company name as a LOGO attribute in the area (recommended), or leave the area blank?
  • L2 The logo stays one per project (Project → Drawing, as today; recommended), or one per template?

3Legend as a table object on a layout (step 5) approval

On a layout tab in Design CAD (a plan sheet's paper), the command LEGEND (LG) or Electrical › Sheets › Legend places a legend table at a point. Its rows are the symbols drawn on this level inside the layout's viewports: lights, sockets, fans, A/C, water heaters, DBs, ELV devices, emergency lights and signs, cable tray and cable routes, keynotes. It is a live object of the sheet: add a device type to the plan and a row appears; delete the last of a type and its row goes. Text at the set's legend height (2.5 mm), 92 mm wide by default, one or two columns.

1The legend table: a paper-space object of the sheet (mm, like a viewport), not drawn objects. Move by its grips; the top-middle grip changes the width; Esc or a click elsewhere drops the pick.
2NOTES placed the same way: the set's general notes with {Parameters}, wrapped to the width. Both switch the sheet's column off ("Legend in: Placed table"), so the drawing gains the 92 mm.
3Rows come from the devices inside the layout's viewports (or the whole level); descriptions from the device palette, editable per project; a row with 0 can be kept or dropped; extra rows typed by hand.

Where it is stored and how it prints

  • Model. DrawingSheet.tables: PlacedTable[] — {kind: 'legend' | 'notes', at [x,y] mm, widthMm, cols, textMm, title, hidden[], extra[], order[]}. One undo step per change; the sheet's hash changes with it, so an issued sheet shows as changed.
  • Print and PDF. sheetRender draws it with the same legendRows / stringLegendSvg the column uses today, at the table's place; the sheet's own column legend is off for that sheet.
  • DWG / DXF. Lines and TEXT on E-LEGEND at the table's place, as the column legend goes out today (editable in any CAD). A TABLE entity is not written: the engine has none yet, and lines and text open everywhere.
  • Freeze. X on the table turns it into drawn objects on E-LEGEND (lines, text at 2.5 mm) and it stops following the plan.
  • Not in step 5. SLD, riser and earthing sheets keep the column or strip legend (they have no layout tab); a legend table on them can follow later through the Sheets page.

Decisions

  • C1 A live sheet object with Freeze (recommended), or drawn objects at once (a snapshot that does not follow the plan)?
  • C2 Rows from the layout's viewports (recommended) or from the whole level by default?
  • C3 NOTES as a placeable table in the same step (recommended; same mechanism), or legend only?
  • C4 DWG as lines and text on E-LEGEND (recommended), or wait for a TABLE entity in the engine?
