Design CAD → Annotate sub-tab — mockup (2026-11-08)

Design CAD's ribbon has two sub-tabs today: LVCAD (Draw, Modify, Annotate, Layers, Properties, Measure) and Electrical, ELV & sheets (Rooms, Cables, Devices, Edit devices, Wiring, Reference, Sheet set, Layouts). Annotate is one group on LVCAD: Text ▾, Dimension ▾, Leader, Room tag, North arrow and Revision cloud. This mockup adds an Annotate sub-tab between them. LVCAD and Electrical keep every tool they have today, so nothing moves. The new sub-tab has the full set (Text, Dimensions, Leaders, Tables, Markup, Annotation scale), plus tables and legends filled from the project's data.

Notes (MT) — on the drawing

GENERAL NOTES
1. All dimensions are in millimetres unless stated otherwise.
2. Socket outlets at 450 mm above FFL; switches at 1200 mm.
3. Final circuits in 2.5 mm² unless noted on the circuit list.
4. Refer to the board schedules for protective devices.

One object: move, copy and erase it as one. Double-click to edit the text on the command line's box. Width handles change where it wraps.

Mask new

Click a closed object, or pick points round an area: a white (paper-colour) fill that hides what lies under it, so a tag or note stays readable over trays, hatches and the architectural underlay. Its outline shows only when picked or when "Show mask frames" is on. Prints white.

Annotation scale later

Text, dimensions, leaders and tables picked get one more plan scale (for example 1:50 as well as 1:100). A layout viewport at 1:50 shows them at their 1:50 size, so the same note prints 2.5 mm high in both viewports.

Not proposed

  • Centre mark / centre line: for machined parts and section drawings; little use on electrical plans.
  • Spell check: needs an offline dictionary; can come later with Find and replace.
  • Link to a spreadsheet file: "From project data" covers what electrical plans need; an Excel link can follow if asked for.
