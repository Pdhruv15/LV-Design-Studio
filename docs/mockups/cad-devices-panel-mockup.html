What happens

  • Plan positions are kept: every level is drawn on the same origin.
  • Each copied light or socket adds a point to its new circuit, as placing it by hand does.
  • Boards are not copied: each level has its own.
  • One undo step for all levels.
A typical level stands for all its floors: it has one plan and one set of devices.
