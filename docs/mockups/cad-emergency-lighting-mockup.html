Design CAD → Plan → Devices → Emergency — mockup (2026-11-03)

The engineer draws the escape route along the corridor centre line (far end to exit), marks points of emphasis (call points, extinguishers, stairs, exit doors) and clicks rooms that need open-area lighting. The app places a fitting at each exit, change of direction and point of emphasis, fills the gaps up to the fitting's spacing, adds exit and direction signs, and checks sign viewing distance and duration (BS 5266-1 / EN 1838). Nothing is read automatically from the architect's drawing.
