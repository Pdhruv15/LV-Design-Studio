Proposed — one dialog, its three tabs

Lines & arrows — styles on the left with their buttons (Import DXF… among them), the preview on paper on the right. Extend beyond dimension line has the cursor, so its two short pieces show in gold in the preview and the line under it names them.

Text — Text height has the cursor: the two lengths show in gold. The body keeps the height of the tallest tab, so OK and Cancel stay where they are.

Units — the preview writes the length as the style will, here with the suffix " m" (in gold while Suffix has the cursor). Prefix and suffix share one row, as do the two options of how the length is written.

What changes (numbers as in Today)

  1. The dialog takes the wide frame (380 px, as Drafting settings and Save As): every tab name and label fits on one line.
  2. Wording: Extend beyond line → Extend beyond dimension line; Line past extension lines → Extend past extension lines, under a new heading Dimension line; the heading Lines → Extension lines.
  3. The style list (five rows) sits beside the preview, with New, Delete, Set current and Import DXF… under it in two rows. The Name box moves above the preview; Standard's stays greyed.
  4. The preview is 188 × 104 px, black on white as on paper, with a horizontal and a vertical dimension (so Text always horizontal and the second arrow show). The part the value with the cursor changes is drawn in gold, and named under it.
  5. A swatch beside Arrow and Second arrow shows the arrow chosen; the lists stay as they are.
  6. One row: Hide extension lines ☐ Line 1 ☐ Line 2.
  7. The body keeps one height on every tab (the tallest, Lines & arrows), so OK and Cancel do not move.
  8. Import DXF… joins New, Delete and Set current; the footer keeps ?, Cancel and OK.
  9. The note becomes one line under the preview (Draws as before until changed); the full sentence is in ?.

Not changed: the fields, their values and units, what each one does, the three tabs and their order, Enter / Esc, moving the dialog by its title, and the dialog's place on screen.
