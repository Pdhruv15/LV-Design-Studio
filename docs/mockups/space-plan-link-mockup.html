Found in the code today

  1. Space plan takes nothing from the Load assessment: it has its own use types (a third density table beside Room types and the density standards), its own settings and its own transformer count.
  2. Its demand is the sum of areas × W/m² × demand factor. Lifts, pumps, EV chargers and fire pumps are in it only when typed as kW rows, and there is no coincidence between categories.
  3. "Automatic" picks 1,000 or 1,500 kVA only. The assessment picks from the Transformers catalogue up to its largest size, with N or N+1.
  4. Transformers are sized in three places (Load assessment, Space plan, Panels → Transformers) and the three figures are never shown side by side.
  5. "Take areas from the rooms" reads loose rooms only: flats placed on levels are left out, the gap fixed for the assessment in v2.230.
  6. Create SLD replaces every board and feeder of the project. The assessment's Create MDB + feeders adds MDBs. The two starts compete, and Create SLD's load feeders do not shrink as DBs are designed.
  7. Substation area is filled from the plan once ("Fill from project"). Nothing says the plan changed afterwards.

Decisions for you

  1. What Space plan is for: the supply arrangement after the assessment: which load on which MDB, transformer and RMU (recommended). Or fold it into the Load assessment page. Or leave the two apart.
  2. Where its loads come from: the assessment's categories and services per building, with "Take areas from the rooms" kept as the other choice (recommended). Or rooms only, as today.
  3. Settings and sizes: follow the assessment's supply settings, with a notice when they differ; keep 1,000 / 1,500 kVA as the default sizes and offer the catalogue sizes (recommended). Or its own settings as today.
  4. Create SLD: becomes Create / Update supply, which keeps what is designed and replaces the assessment's own MDBs (recommended). Or keeps replacing everything.
  5. Substation area: a notice and one press to follow the plan (recommended). Or filled by hand as today.
  6. Name: keep "Space plan" for now (recommended). Or rename it "Supply plan", which says what it gives.
  7. Counting fix first: flats in "Take areas from the rooms" need no screen change. Do it now, ahead of the rest (recommended).
