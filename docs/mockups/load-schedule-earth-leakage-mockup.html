Load distribution schedule — earth leakage: ELCB sizes, board schemes and devices (agreed 2026-11-23)

Agreed plan (2026-11-23). Every ELCB on the DB is its own device: click one to make it 3, 6, 9 or 12 circuits, and only that ELCB and its neighbour change. The board-bar list becomes "New ELCBs", the size for ways that have no ELCB yet, and never re-cuts the DB; Re-group all ELCBs… does that after showing before and after. When the app makes ELCBs it keeps 30 mA and 100 mA circuits apart. Then more devices for one circuit (4P RCBO, No RCD, AFDD + RCBO, MCB + RCD add-on block), the RCD type (AC / A / F / B) and the whole-board schemes. No country is applied by default.

Today in the app (v2.262)
  1. ELCB per (board bar) is a rule for the whole DB: the app cuts the ways into groups of 3 or 6 circuits and sizes one 4P ELCB per group. It does not add a device, and there is no 9.
  2. Add → ELCB / RCD → 3-phase ELCB over ways… already adds one ELCB over any ways you type: Way 3 to way 5 is a 9-circuit ELCB. So 9 works today, but only by typing way numbers.
  3. 1-phase RCBO replaces one circuit's MCB. A 3-phase circuit cannot have an RCBO, and there is no AFDD, RCD add-on block, RCD type (AC / A / F / B), whole-board ELCB or "no RCD" circuit.

"Way" in this app = R + Y + B of one row number = 3 circuits. A UK "9-way" board means 9 single-phase circuits, which is 3 ways here. So every size below is named by circuits first.

5Agreed plan and build steps

Agreed 2026-11-23 ("ok go ahead" to the recommended plan)
  1. 1Every ELCB is its own device. Click its cell and pick 3, 6, 9 or 12 circuits; only that ELCB and its neighbour change.
  2. 2New ELCBs in the board bar is only the size for ways with no ELCB yet. Re-group all ELCBs… re-cuts the DB after showing before and after.
  3. 3When the app makes ELCBs it keeps 30 mA and 100 mA apart; one odd circuit in a way gets Make RCBO in the Check column.
  4. 4Devices: 4P RCBO and No RCD first, then AFDD + RCBO and MCB + RCD add-on block.
  5. 5RCD type AC / A / F / B set from the load, printed only when it is not Type A.
  6. 6Whole-board schemes (one ELCB at the incomer, RCBO on every circuit) last, in Re-group.
  7. 7No country rule by default. Old projects open with the ELCBs they print today.
Build steps (one PR each)
  1. 1ELCB as its own device: click to resize, New ELCBs 3 / 6 / 9 / 12, Re-group all ELCBs…, Balance phases inside each ELCB.
  2. 2Keep 30 mA and 100 mA apart and Make RCBO for one odd circuit.
  3. 3Devices: 4P RCBO and No RCD; then AFDD + RCBO and MCB + RCD add-on block. PDF, Excel, BOQ, enclosure list and earth fault check follow.
  4. 4RCD type on every ELCB and RCBO, counted in the BOQ.
  5. 5Whole-board schemes in Re-group; S-type at the incomer when 30 mA devices sit below it.

Each step adds tests for grouping, numbering, ratings and exports.
