Load distribution schedule — earth leakage: ELCB sizes, board schemes and devices (proposal, 2026-11-23)

"ELCB per" in the board bar is not a device: it is the rule the app uses to cut the whole DB into ELCB groups. This proposal lets you add an ELCB in the size you want — 3, 6, 9 or 12 circuits — with one click, adds the board schemes used in other countries (one ELCB at the incomer, an RCBO on every circuit), and adds the other earth leakage devices: 4P RCBO for 3-phase circuits, AFDD + RCBO, MCB + RCD add-on block, a circuit with no RCD, and the RCD type (AC / A / F / B). No country is applied by default.

Today in the app (v2.262)
  1. ELCB per (board bar) is a rule for the whole DB: the app cuts the ways into groups of 3 or 6 circuits and sizes one 4P ELCB per group. It does not add a device, and there is no 9.
  2. Add → ELCB / RCD → 3-phase ELCB over ways… already adds one ELCB over any ways you type: Way 3 to way 5 is a 9-circuit ELCB. So 9 works today, but only by typing way numbers.
  3. 1-phase RCBO replaces one circuit's MCB. A 3-phase circuit cannot have an RCBO, and there is no AFDD, RCD add-on block, RCD type (AC / A / F / B), whole-board ELCB or "no RCD" circuit.

"Way" in this app = R + Y + B of one row number = 3 circuits. A UK "9-way" board means 9 single-phase circuits, which is 3 ways here. So every size below is named by circuits first.

What the menu does
  1. aSize buttons add a 4P ELCB in one click, starting at the selected circuit's way (or the first way with no device placed by hand). They fit up to the last used way; the ELCB number follows way order (ELCB-1, 2, …), as today.
  2. b9 and 12 circuits are also in the board scheme list (2), so a whole DB can be cut into 3-way or 4-way groups automatically.
  3. cAFDD + RCBO is one RCBO with arc fault detection (IEC 62606); it prints as "RCBO-3 · 20 A 30 mA 2P + AFDD" and counts as one device in the BOQ.
  4. dMCB + RCD add-on block: for ratings or curves where an RCBO is not made; prints both and counts both.
  5. eNo RCD is for a circuit that must not trip with others (fire alarm panel, smoke extract). The reason prints in the ELCB cell; the earth fault check then has to pass on the MCB alone.
  6. f4P RCBO gives a 3-phase circuit (EV charger, pump) its own MCB + earth leakage in one device instead of an ELCB and an MCB.
  7. gA circuit still has one earth leakage device only (your rule, 2026-11-18): ELCB group, RCBO, AFDD + RCBO, add-on block or none.

5Decisions for you, and build steps

Decisions (my recommendation first)
  1. A. Board bar: rename "ELCB per" to Earth leakage with the scheme list in 2 (recommended), or keep "ELCB per" and only add 9 and 12?
  2. B. Sizes: 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 circuits + Other (recommended), or only 3 / 6 / 9?
  3. C. A size button starts at the selected circuit's way (recommended), or always at the first way with no device placed by hand?
  4. D. New devices: 4P RCBO, No RCD and RCD type first (recommended), then AFDD + RCBO and MCB + RCD add-on block? Or all together?
  5. E. Print the RCD type in the ELCB cell of the PDF and Excel form ("25 A 30 mA 4P Type A")? Recommended yes, black and white, only when the type is not the default A — or always?
  6. F. Country rules: none by default (recommended); optional design rule "most circuits per RCD" (e.g. 3 or 8) that only warns in the Check column?
  7. G. "One ELCB at the incomer": one cell over all rows in the ELCB column and "+ ELCB 100 A 30 mA 4P" after the incomer (recommended); S-type offered when 30 mA devices sit below it?
Build steps after approval (one PR each)
  1. 1Sizes. ELCB per 9 and 12 circuits; size buttons in the ELCB / RCD menu and the dialog. The data already allows any ways by hand; only the board rule gains 9 and 12.
  2. 2Devices. 4P RCBO for 3-phase circuits, No RCD (with reason), AFDD + RCBO, MCB + RCD add-on block: one "earth leakage device" kind on the circuit in place of today's RCBO flag (old projects read as before). PDF, Excel, BOQ, enclosure list and earth fault check follow.
  3. 3RCD type. AC / A / F / B on every ELCB and RCBO, Auto by load type (EV B, inverter AC F, otherwise A), printed per decision E, counted in the BOQ.
  4. 4Schemes. One ELCB at the incomer and RCBO on every circuit in the Earth leakage list; S-type at the incomer per decision G.

Each step: tests for grouping, ratings, numbering and exports; Balance phases keeps circuits under devices placed by hand in their ways, as today.
