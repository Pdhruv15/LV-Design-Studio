Found in the code today

  1. The link is two copy buttons (GFA from Building, From Building rooms). After the press, nothing says that Building changed.
  2. Only the first building is read; a second tower or a podium is left out.
  3. Flats placed on levels are not counted, only loose rooms: in the assessment's area schedule and in Building's own Load figure.
  4. The car park area ignores the Floors count of a repeated basement level, and is 0 when the basement has a gross area but no Car park rooms.
  5. Two density tables. A room type with no matching name gets a guessed split (20 % lighting, 80 % power, cooling 100 W/m²) and loses the Dwelling tick, so it adds no water heating or cooking.
  6. Nothing comes back: Building never shows the assessment, and Common services starts from its own rules (here 3 lifts where the assessment has 6; no EV chargers, pool or façade).
  7. Create MDB + feeders leaves one lump feeder per category. DBs and services generated under the same MDB are added on top, so the load is counted twice until the lump feeders are deleted by hand.

Decisions for you

  1. Link or combine? Keep two pages, linked both ways (recommended). Or one page with two tabs. Building also feeds drawings, risers, earthing and ELV, so it stays a page of its own.
  2. When Building changes: a notice and one press to update, with the result shown first (recommended); your typed figures never move on their own. Or the assessment always follows Building and those cells are locked.
  3. Several buildings: one assessment for the buildings you tick (recommended now). Or one assessment per building, a larger step for later.
  4. Densities: an "Assessment space" column on Room types (recommended). Or one merged density table for both pages.
  5. Common services start from the assessment and replace its lump feeders (recommended), or stay separate.
  6. Counting fixes first? Items 2 to 5 on the left need no screen change. Do them now, ahead of the rest (recommended).
