Found in the code today

  1. Four transformer size lists. Load assessment: the catalogue, else 500 to 2,500 kVA in 8 sizes. Space plan: 1,000 / 1,500, or the market list (with 1,500, without 200). Tx & Gen sizing: "DEWA" 500 / 1,000 / 1,500, or "IEC" (with 200, without 1,500). The same name means two different lists.
  2. Two generator size lists: 22 sizes here (20 to 2,500 kVA), 11 on Load assessment (100 to 2,500 kVA) unless the Generators catalogue has sizes.
  3. Growth and loading are held in three places: the assessment's supply settings, the plan's settings, and the project design settings this study uses. Nothing shows when they differ.
  4. The generator is sized twice (Load assessment, this study) and the two figures are never shown together. The transformers table added in v2.243 is on Space plan and Panels → Transformers, not on this page.
  5. The assessment's generator goes nowhere: the emergency boards on Panels start with a blank generator.
  6. Fix waiting to be merged (PR 747, v2.247): after Create / Update supply the plan's load feeders had no essential mark, so this study's generator came out too small.

Decisions for you

  1. Size lists: one definition with the same three choices on the three pages (recommended). Or each page keeps its own.
  2. DEWA list: 500, 1,000 and 1,500 kVA on every page (recommended, as this page has it). Or 1,000 and 1,500 kVA only, as Space plan has it.
  3. Market standard list: one list of 16 sizes, with both 200 and 1,500 kVA (recommended). Or tell me the sizes you want.
  4. Growth and loading: shown side by side with a notice, nothing changed for you (recommended): the concept figures and the design figures may differ on purpose. Or one press to take the assessment's.
  5. Generator sizes: one list for both pages: the Generators catalogue, else the 22 standard sizes (recommended). Or keep two.
  6. Tables: the transformers table and a generator table on this page (recommended). Or leave the page as it is.
  7. Emergency boards: start with the generator from this study, else the assessment's (recommended). Or blank as today.
