Either way inSchedule in the app's table or in your sheet. Both write to the same project, so the SLD and the documents are the same.
Rules are applied onceThe authority's rules size and check every circuit when it reaches the project, whichever way it came in.
What a sheet must haveOne row per circuit, a panel number and a Fed from cell. Layouts with two circuits on one row, or several panels on one sheet, need a later step.
