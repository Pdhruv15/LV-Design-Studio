B. A colour per ELV system

Fire alarmSecurityDataSoundOther

Adds four or five colours and needs a legend; some pairs clash for colour-blind sight.

C. A mark on ELV symbols

A thin ring round the symbol: no clash, but weaker at a glance, and it changes known symbols.

What changes, what stays

Changes (screen only)
  • ELV devices on the plan
  • Coverage circles
  • ELV items of the side palette and the gallery
Stays
  • LV devices, selection, snap, sketch and the accent
  • Plots and PDFs stay black-and-white
  • Saved DWG/DXF files are not changed

Decisions

  • F1. Which option: A (recommended), B or C.
  • F2. Which shade for A: rose (recommended) or violet (weaker for green-weak sight).
  • F3. Coverage circles follow the ELV colour: yes (recommended) or keep them in the accent colour.
