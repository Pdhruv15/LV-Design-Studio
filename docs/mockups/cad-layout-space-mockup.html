Design CAD → Model and layouts — mockup

As in industry CAD: you draw once in Model (real size, as today), and each sheet is a layout: a paper with its title block, and one or more viewports looking into the model at a scale. One model of a level gives its lighting, power, ELV and containment sheets.

new in this proposal · 2026-11-07 · 2D only · sheets stay black and white

A row of tabs under the drawing: Model, then one tab per layout of this plan (its sheet number), then + for a new layout. Right-click a layout tab: Rename, Copy, Move, Delete, Page setup.

Dashed purple = viewport frames (on screen only; they do not print). The notes, legend, key-plan label and title block are on the paper, in mm. The plan inside the viewport is the model itself: change the model and every layout shows it.

A layout is a plan sheet of the drawing register: its number, revisions, issues and PDF / DXF export work as today. Today's automatic plan sheets become layouts with one viewport each (split levels keep their match lines), so nothing is lost.

8Decisions for you

  1. Tabs: Model / layout tabs under the drawing, as above? (recommended) Or a fourth ribbon sub-tab “Layouts”.
  2. Sheets: a layout is a plan sheet in the register (one list, one numbering)? (recommended)
  3. Today's plan sheets: turn them into layouts automatically, keeping their numbers and revisions? (recommended)
  4. Viewport frames: not printed by default? (recommended)
  5. On the paper first: text, notes, leaders, clouds, lines, rectangles, legend, north arrow; more later? (recommended)
  6. Save DWG / DXF: write the layouts as real paper-space layouts with viewports in phase 2, after the screens work? (recommended)
