Design CAD → Plan → Devices → Light room — mockup (2026-11-03)

Choose Light room (LUX), point at a room the engineer has already picked or traced, and the app counts the luminaires by the lumen method for that outline (L-shapes and angled walls too), shows them on the plan as a preview, and places them as light points on the chosen circuit. Nothing is read automatically from the architect's drawing; the room outline is the one already on the plan.
