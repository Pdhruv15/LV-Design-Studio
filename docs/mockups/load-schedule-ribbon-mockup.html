Check goes to the next circuit that fails or needs attention, in this schedule first and then in the next board that has one, and says why. The reasons and the fix are the ones the SLD already gives; nothing new is calculated. The voltage drop column comes from Columns.

Run and checkRun shows the number of circuits out of date under its name, as on the SLD tab. Check is new here: today the Check column says OK or Fail, with no reason and no way to step to the next problem (4).
ScheduleThe three buttons are unchanged. Previous board and Next board step through the schedules in the order of the board chips on the page.
Circuits and loads · RowsAdd circuit, 3-phase circuit, Spare and Space are the buttons above the grid today. Add load is unchanged. Fill down, Edit and Delete work today from Ctrl/⌘ D, a double-click and the bin at the end of the row. Duplicate, Move up and Move down do not exist yet.
Size and balanceBalance phases is the button above the grid, with the imbalance under its name. Re-size clears "Sized by hand" on the chosen rows, so MCB, wire and ECC follow the load again; today that is one circuit at a time, in the circuit dialog.
Copy and undo · ShowCopy, Paste, Undo and Redo work today from the keyboard only. Copy from board fills this schedule from another board's, for typical floors. Columns adds result columns on screen; the exported authority form does not change. Find looks in every schedule.
Output · narrow windowsExport gathers the export buttons of the page header. Import reads circuits from an Excel or CSV list; today only paste from Excel works. On a narrow window the groups that do not fit move into More, as on the SLD tab.
