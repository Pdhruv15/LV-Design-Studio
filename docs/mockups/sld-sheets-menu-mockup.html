SLD page — a Sheets menu at the end of the diagram tabs

Proposal of 2026-11-19 (UI UX proposals - 2026-11-19 - SLD sheets menu.md). The sheets stay in Design CAD → Sheets, as decided on 2026-10-29. The SLD page gets one way into them, next to the four diagram tabs. Each item opens Design CAD → Sheets at that sheet or register tab. Example project: MDB-1 with SMDB-FF, MCC-1, DB-EV and DB-PV; two SLD sheets and one riser sheet.

Opens Design CAD → Sheets. Paper, scale and pens: Page setups there.

The button sits right after the four tabs, apart from them, because it opens another page. Its tooltip: Sheets of this diagram and the sheet set, in Design CAD → Sheets. The first group lists the sheets that show what is on screen; the second group is the register tabs that the Design CAD ribbon already has (All sheets, Layout, Title block, Issues). "Sheet setup" is the Layout tab, under the name the item had on the SLD before v2.4.
